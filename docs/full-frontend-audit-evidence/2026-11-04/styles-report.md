The previous route matrix has 160 `color-contrast` incomplete groups representing 7,038 node appearances at 1440px. Those are incomplete checks, not passes, and this review does not automatically clear them. Base-token tests also do not establish the current Aero cascade's rendered contrast.

Four current representative scans (home Night 390, home Day 1440, About Night 1440 and About Day 390) produced 233 incomplete nodes: 194 attributed by axe to a pseudo element and 39 to a gradient. The manual sampling covers the visible text portions of those representative cases, including reading prose, buttons, headings, Atlas text, wallpaper labels, tray text and captions. The final measurement for that initial audit pass includes 240 rendered text runs. This preceded the later rounded-bar follow-up, whose separate 28-run painted-background samples are reported above. All eligible sampled runs meet their 4.5:1 normal-text / 3:1 large-text threshold in those particular captures; the lowest sampled ratio is 5.567:1. Some initial axe selectors refer to a changed React-generated ID or to offscreen/non-text content, so this is not a claim that every axe node was individually cleared. The separate busy-summary and coarse-hover state probes test interactions absent from the resting axe scan. See [glyph-contrast-final-measurements.json](styles-evidence/glyph-contrast-final-measurements.json), [contrast-measurements.json](styles-evidence/contrast-measurements.json) and [caption-measurements.json](styles-evidence/caption-measurements.json).

Sampling method: wait for fonts and settled entry rendering; record actual text ranges and computed foreground/ancestor opacity; capture the rendered page, a capture with text fill hidden but its shadow retained, and one with both fill and shadow hidden. Inspect solid interior glyph pixels within 3 RGB units of the expected foreground, excluding matches with the empty background. Compare foreground to the plain backdrop and to an opposing text halo where present. For locale-code opacity, sample the composited backdrop with decorative SVGs hidden and blend the foreground at its actual group opacity. No conclusion is based solely on literal colour values.

These are finite DPR-1 Chromium/Linux screenshots, not a normative all-font/all-state accessibility certification. The initially broad text-rectangle samples included fixed taskbar occlusion and treated a dark glyph's own dark shadow as background, creating false low ratios. They are retained as diagnostic `incomplete-measurements.json`; the glyph-based measurements supersede them. The original probe also had one hidden retained-footer hover error, and the preference probe initially ran before hydration; both were corrected and rerun. No product defect is inferred from those harness errors.
