# Apple 2026 Research for the ResearchOS Redesign

**Research date:** 2026-10-16

## Method

- Official sources were opened with WebFetch by Haiku 5.5 research agents, orchestrated by Claude Opus 5.5.
- Seven lanes ran: foundations, visual, interaction, a11y-rtl, wwdc26, webkit, licensing-perf.
- Where a plain developer.apple.com URL returned only the page title, the `.md` variant was read in full.
- Pages read in more than one lane are merged into one row. The row keeps the highest priority recorded.
- Quotes are short, attributed excerpts. Sources are recorded as verified only where the research read the page text.
- The research data verified no repository file paths. "Affected files/areas" names UI surfaces and the existing commits cited in the research. Check paths before editing.

## Design interpretation

This section is my synthesis of the sources above, not guidance stated by any source. Each principle cites the evidence it draws on.

1. **Hierarchy first.** The floating reading window is the single dominant surface. The dock and search dialog are visibly secondary. (Design principles; Layout.)
2. **Two layers.** Chrome (dock, title bars, search dialog, toolbars) is the functional layer. Project copy, paper abstracts and reading text sit on a plain, more opaque content surface. (Materials; WWDC25 219.)
3. **Glass sparingly.** Limit translucent treatment to a few chrome elements per screen. Never place glass over glass. Always provide an opaque fallback for reduced transparency. (Materials; Adopting Liquid Glass; MDN reduced-transparency.)
4. **One accent, one meaning.** Cyan #06b6d4 marks primary actions, the selected item and active status only. Any state shown in colour also carries a shape, icon or text label. (Branding; Color; Accessibility.)
5. **Semantic tokens with three variants.** Define light, dark and increased-contrast values as custom properties on :root. Measure each text and background pair against 4.5:1, and 7:1 for small body and mono metadata. (Color; Dark Mode; WCAG 1.4.3.)
6. **Mirror by language, not by decoration.** Set dir per locale. Flip navigation and progress controls. Keep the logo, photos, checkmarks, DOIs, years and counts unflipped and left-to-right. (Right to left; Layout.)
7. **Recoverable and predictable.** Every overlay has a visible close or back control and Escape handling. Locale and theme switches keep the open window and selection. The dock never fully hides a focused element. (Design principles; WCAG 2.4.11.)
8. **Keyboard and focus are part of the visual design.** Use :focus-visible rings. Keep targets at least 24 CSS px and aim for 44 px on touch. Every dock, explorer and dialog action is reachable without a pointer. (Accessibility; WCAG 2.1.1, 2.4.7, 2.5.8; Safari 27.)
9. **Motion is optional and subordinate to text.** The loader is skippable and does not gate content. The rain is decorative and easy to disable. No motion on every hover. Reduced motion is honoured through a general web convention, not a verified HIG rule. (Motion; Accessibility; Safari 27.)
10. **Text grows and reflows.** Use relative units, test at 200 per cent zoom, stack rows at narrow widths, reflow at 320 CSS px, use loose leading for passages of three or more lines, and keep to one or two typefaces per locale. (Typography; Layout; WCAG 1.4.10, 1.4.12.)
11. **Performance is a design requirement.** Effects must not push LCP past 2.5 s or CLS above 0.1. INP (200 ms) is verified in the field, not by Lighthouse alone. (Web Vitals; thresholds article.)
