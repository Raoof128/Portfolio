## Scope and method

This was a source-copy audit of the portfolio, not a live-site or browser review. I inventoried the localized route tree under `src/app/[locale]` (home, about, project list and detail, lab list and detail, writing list and detail, résumé, contact, security policy, hall of fame, not-found, and locale layout/template), then reviewed their authored copy sources: `src/i18n/locales/{en,fa,ar,es,zh}.ts`, `src/lib/data.ts`, `src/lib/project-content/`, `src/lib/writing-translations/`, `src/lib/page-support-copy.ts`, `src/lib/resume-section-copy.ts`, `src/components/os/`, `public/llms.txt`, and the `src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts` generator. Direct route JSX was checked for additional authored strings; it primarily renders the shared dictionaries and content maps.

The English pass applied the Stop Slop workflow to authored prose: remove filler and generic contrast framing, prefer direct verbs and shorter sentences, remove em dashes from prose, and preserve voice, technical meaning, names, exact quotations, disclosures, URLs, identifiers, code, hedges, and claims. The full skill and preserving-voice guidance are installed at `/Users/raoof.r12/.codex/skills/stop-slop/`. For Australian spelling, I followed the [Australian Government Style Manual spelling guidance](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/grammar-punctuation-and-conventions/spelling): use a consistent Australian dictionary while retaining established specialist terms and official names.
